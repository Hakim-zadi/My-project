STORY +
Toi et Moi (aka 'You and I', en français s'il vous plaît)
rose to fame thanks to Napoleon's proposal to
Josephine in 1796. While that's all very romantic, we
like to think of this ring as a symbol of modern love,
as in, the love you have for yourself. Why? Bc all
data shows that when you take care of yourself, you
are in fact a better human for the world around you.
May this serve as a reminder to book that night off,
say no to that dinner you are dreading, eat those
carbs, take that vacay, read that book, or do
whatever it is that *you* need. The rest will thank
you.

Don't let the name fool you, Toi et Moi is all about
*you* and we designed it as such. You're in the
driver's seat here and can select (1) over a dozen
gemstones in pear and emerald cuts, (2) vermeil,
14K, or 18K gold, and (3) a classic or vintage setting.
Full array of options here.
SPECS +

MATERIALS +

Vermeil
Made with a 100% sterling silver base and plated in
a minimum of 2.5 microns of 14k recycled gold or
higher (vs. the standard of 10k gold). We proudly
steer clear from brass, regular gold plating, and
actually stand by the official vermeil 2.5 micron
guidelines (which is 500% thicker, fyi). Because we
like to overachieve, we've also added a bonus anti-
tarnish coat to protect against day-to-day wear.

14k and 18k Solid Gold
We recommend 14k or 18k solid recycled gold if
you're looking for your jewelry to last generations to
come. Solid gold is guaranteed to never tarnish, so
you never have to take it off (bonus: you can wear it
in water). It's 100% worry free. In addition to our
quality standards, all our manufacturers certify
where their gold comes from so we can proudly
stand behind our ethical practices.

Gemstones
Our team of obsessors hand pick the highest
quality, precious and semi-precious gemstones.
Each is unique, but all pass our meticulous
standards.

Recycled Gold
Proudly made with recycled gold. This means the
majority of the gold we use does not come from a
mine, making our jewelry more sustainable. While it
might cost us more, we vote planet > profit, all day
every day.
